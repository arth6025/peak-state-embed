Who it's for

The ICP is a community fitness organiser in an Indian metro that runs recurring paid events and has a website it wants to grow, such as the three pilots: Find Your Fit, Sisters in Sweat and Cubbon Classic 10K.

Not for V1: direct integrations with timing hardware (V1 assigns bibs and exports runners to the timing partner instead), and organisers without a website.

The problem

  • Traffic leaks. Linking out sends every searcher to another domain, so the club builds no search equity of its own.
  • Iframe embeds are invisible. Tools like KonfHub's embed drop an iframe; Google credits the vendor, and AI crawlers, which mostly don't run JavaScript, see nothing.
  • No attribution. Clubs can't fire their Meta pixel or GA4 on a booking, so they can't tell which posts and ads sell.

Every event must exist as fast, server-rendered HTML on the club's domain, marked up so Google and AI answer engines can read, rank and quote it.

Change from the first draft: server-rendered hosted pages move from V2 into V1. A JS-only embed doesn't meet the AEO bar.

How it works

  • Checkout opens as a sheet over the club's page and sends ps:checkout_started, ps:payment_success and other events to it, which the page pushes to dataLayer.
  • Mobile payments: UPI intent and 3-D Secure open in a top-level peakst8 tab, then return to the club's page.
  • Security: checkout can only be framed by the club's verified domains; webhooks are HMAC-signed.

FAQ

What is peakst8 Embed?
It puts a club's events and the full peakst8 booking flow on the club's own website. Event pages live on the club's domain; peakst8 runs tickets, payments, refunds and check-in.
Does it help a club's SEO?
Yes. Each event gets a server-rendered page on the club's domain with Event and FAQ schema, a sitemap entry and an IndexNow ping, so Google credits the club, not peakst8.
Will ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews show a club's events?
Pages are plain HTML that AI crawlers can read, open with date, place and price, carry an FAQ, and are listed in the club's llms.txt, which makes them easy to quote and cite.
Who handles payments and refunds?
peakst8. The club sees every order in its dashboard and gets it by webhook.
How long does setup take?
The target is under 10 minutes: switch-on, pick a mode, then point a subdomain or paste one snippet.

Open questions
